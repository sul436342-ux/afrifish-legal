Purchases and restored entitlements

In-app purchases are processed by Google Play or the Apple App Store. Permanent non-consumable entitlements may be recoverable with the same store account through the in-game restore function. Spent consumable balances and local game progress are not guaranteed to transfer between devices because AfriFish does not currently provide its own cloud-save account system.

Privacy requests

For privacy questions, a copy of the policy, or requests involving information you sent directly to us by email, contact the same address. Save data that exists only on your device is generally controlled by deleting the app or clearing app data through your device. Third-party store or advertising records may need to be managed through those providers.
