Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 12, 2026 · Applies to AfriFish on Android and iOS

This Privacy Policy explains how AfriFish, a casual mobile fishing game distributed under the QQGame developer label ("we", "us"), handles information when you use the game.

1. Scope

This policy applies to AfriFish on Android and iOS. The core fishing game is designed to work offline. Optional online features may include rewarded advertising, app-store purchases and privacy consent requests. The game does not currently require a QQGame account, online multiplayer account or developer-operated cloud-save account.

2. Information processed

Information stored on your device

AfriFish stores game progress locally, which can include your player ID or locally selected name and avatar, levels, fish discoveries, equipment, inventory, virtual currency, achievements, aquarium progress, preferences and local purchase-entitlement or transaction-recovery records. This information is used to run and restore your game on the same device. We do not operate a game account server that routinely receives these saved-game records.

Advertising-related information

When optional rewarded ads are available and you choose to use them, Google AdMob and its advertising partners may process information such as advertising or device identifiers (where permitted), IP address, approximate location derived from technical signals, ad interactions, app or device details, and diagnostics. The exact data collected and its use can depend on your device, region, applicable consent and the ad networks involved. We do not claim that rewarded advertising is free of third-party data processing.

In-app purchases

Purchases are processed by the Apple App Store or Google Play, not directly by our own payment server. Those stores handle your payment credentials under their own policies. AfriFish may process store product identifiers, receipts or transaction identifiers, purchase status and locally stored purchase records to grant entitlements, avoid duplicate rewards, support recovery, and help resolve purchase issues. We do not collect your complete payment card details through the game.

Support messages

If you contact us by email, we process the information you choose to provide, such as your email address, message, device or app information, and transaction references needed to investigate your request. Please do not send payment passwords or full card details.

3. How information is used

  • To operate the game and save your progress on your device.
  • To enable optional rewarded advertisements, subject to applicable consent and platform controls.
  • To process and recover in-app purchases and prevent duplicate fulfillment.
  • To respond to support, safety and purchase-related requests.
  • To comply with applicable legal obligations and help prevent fraud or abuse.

4. Third-party services

The release version may use the following services. Their collection and processing practices are governed by their own policies:

  • Google AdMob / Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) — optional rewarded advertising and consent management. Google Privacy Policy.
  • Google Play — Android app distribution and in-app purchases. Google Privacy Policy.
  • Apple App Store — iOS app distribution and in-app purchases. Apple Privacy Policy.

We do not describe these service providers as collecting nothing. Their data practices and retention periods may differ from locally stored game data. We do not sell players’ locally stored save files to data brokers.

5. Your privacy choices

Rewarded ads are initiated voluntarily. Where required, the game uses a consent-management flow before requesting ads, and makes a Privacy choices option available when the consent provider requires it. You may also manage advertising identifiers and tracking permissions through your device settings, subject to your platform and regional rules. Refusing applicable advertising consent does not prevent the core offline fishing game from working.

You can stop using the game at any time. Removing the app or clearing its app data generally deletes locally stored save data from your device; it may not erase records retained independently by Apple, Google or other service providers. For questions about data we receive through support email, contact us using the details below.

6. Data retention and security

Game progress stored on your device remains there until it is overwritten, deleted through platform controls or the app is removed, subject to any device backup behavior. Support communications are retained only as reasonably necessary to handle the request, meet legal obligations or resolve disputes. App stores and advertising partners maintain their own retention policies.

We use platform and SDK security features intended to protect communications and purchases, but no method of storage or transmission can be guaranteed completely secure.

7. Children and family privacy

AfriFish does not include a developer-operated public chat, social profile directory or account-registration system. We do not ask children to submit personal information through an in-game registration flow. Parents or guardians who have questions about a child's use of the game or advertising privacy can contact us. Any child-directed audience settings and relevant advertising protections must be configured according to the actual audience and applicable laws before release.

8. International processing

Because AfriFish may be offered internationally and uses global app-store and advertising providers, information processed by those providers may be transferred to or processed in countries other than your own, subject to applicable safeguards and the providers’ privacy policies.

9. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as game features, third-party services or legal requirements change. We will update the date at the top of this page when a revision takes effect. Material changes may also be communicated within the game when appropriate.
